Being playtest stickers, the cards have no art, artist credits, or Collector number , and are marked with internal card codes &#91; 1 &#93; . Playtest cards are not considered to be “real” cards nor are they considered silver-bordered . They were more meant as entertainment than as game pieces. &#91; 2 &#93; &#91; 3 &#93; The playtest cards haven't undergone rules scrutiny, and Play Design hasn't tested them rigorously for balance. &#91; 4 &#93; Philadelphia 2023 cards A total of 62 cards were designed for this event. &#91; 5 &#93; Card name Code Card text Notes Unclaimed Cat CW02 Creature — Cat If you're on the Mirran team, Unclaimed Cat has lifelink .
