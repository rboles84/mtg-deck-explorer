Description

The event cards feature playtest stickers designed by Gavin Verhey and hand-placed by Gavin and Wizards staff, looking and functioning like the playtest cards of the Mystery Booster set. Being playtest stickers, the cards have no art, artist credits, or Collector number, and are marked with internal card codes[1].

Playtest cards are not considered to be “real” cards nor are they considered silver-bordered. They were more meant as entertainment than as game pieces.[2][3] The playtest cards haven't undergone rules scrutiny, and Play Design hasn't tested them rigorously for balance.[4]

Minneapolis 2023 cards

In contrast to the Philadelphia event cards, Minneapolis cards have a regular, colored card frame, colored mana symbols in their casting cost, a rarity indicator where the expansion symbol would normally be located, and a Power/Toughness box. A total of 62 new cards were designed for this event, as well as 11 planes.[7]
