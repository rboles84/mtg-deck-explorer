Alchemy: Reality Fracture has been summoned to deliver more distorted dispatches from Jace's Echoverse! This digital-only release contains 31 cards brimming with the power and intrigue of a splintered multiverse. Alchemy: Reality Fracture will be available in the MTG Arena Store tomorrow, October 13.
