Scryfall statistics 4 cards 25% &#32; 50% &#32; 25% &#32; Actualize is a designed-for-digital , MTG Arena -specific ability word introduced in Alchemy: Reality Fracture . Contents 1 Description 2 Rules 3 Example 4 References Description Actualize triggers whenever you cast a spell that isn't from your starting deck . The unkeyworded form of this mechanic was previously seen on Ursine Guide &#32;( Alchemy: Strixhaven ). Actualize works best with other digital mechanics like conjure (cards created into your hand, library, or battlefield) and heist (stealing a card from your opponent's deck). Rules Actualize is not included as part of the Comprehensive Rules . &#91; 1 &#93; It operates according to the programmed rules of Magic: The Gathering Arena . Example Example Willbound Quill Artifact Starting intensity 1 : Add one mana of any color. Actualize — Whenever you cast a spell that isn’t from your starting deck, copy that spell.
