Scryfall statistics 1 card 100% &#32; Covercast is a designed-for-digital , MTG Arena -specific mechanic introduced in Alchemy: Strixhaven . The mechanic is a play on triggered abilities that allows them to trigger from any zone. Unlike most triggered abilities, these abilities trigger in secret, without using the stack, meaning cards with the mechanic will not be prematurely revealed before being played. Contents 1 Description 2 Rules 3 Example 4 See also 5 References Description Currently, Summitfest Closing Ceremony is the only card to use the Covercast mechanic. It also makes use of the Intensity mechanic, allowing it to begin intensifying from the beginning of the game rather than needing to wait to be drawn or played. It triggers whenever the card's owner spends five or more mana to cast an instant or sorcery spell, playing into Prismari 's Opus mechanic in the base Secrets of Strixhaven set. Because the stack is omitted from this wording, it means that the card does not need to be on the battlefield for its "Whenever you cast..." trigger to work. &#91; 1 &#93; Its Intensity will increase even if the card is in your hand , in your library , in your graveyard , or in exile . Rules The Covercast mechanic is not included in the Comprehensive Rules . It operates according to the programmed rules of Magic: The Gathering Arena . Example Example Summitfest Closing Ceremony Instant Starting Intensity 3 Covercast — Whenever you cast another instant or sorcery spell, if five or more mana was spent to cast it, this card intensifies.
