Actualize is a designed-for-digital, MTG Arena-specific ability word introduced in Alchemy: Reality Fracture.

Contents

  • 1 Description
  • 2 Rules
  • 3 Example
  • 4 References

Description

Actualize triggers whenever you cast a spell that isn't from your starting deck. The unkeyworded form of this mechanic was previously seen on Ursine Guide (Alchemy: Strixhaven).

Actualize works best with other digital mechanics like conjure (cards created into your hand, library, or battlefield) and heist (stealing a card from your opponent's deck).

Rules

Actualize is not included as part of the Comprehensive Rules.[1] It operates according to the programmed rules of Magic: The Gathering Arena.

Example

Example

Willbound Quill
Artifact
Starting intensity 1
: Add one mana of any color.
Actualize — Whenever you cast a spell that isn’t from your starting deck, copy that spell. You may choose new targets for the copy.
