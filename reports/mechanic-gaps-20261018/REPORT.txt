WHAT IS STILL MISSING
329 current labels have official references; 312 have approved searches. The completed sourcing pass remains complete within that scope.
The retained October 17 Scryfall Oracle corpus contains 891 distinct reported labels across 38,753 cards. 562 labels have no current reference; 488 occur on paper Commander-legal cards. 526 of the 562 occur on exactly one corpus card. These are provider labels, NOT 562 verified universal mechanics. Most of this long tail needs card-specific verification, and every unverified row is marked in gap-audit.json.
The three current Scryfall catalogs have 62 names without references. These overlap the corpus gaps and include unused keyword entries, ordinary game actions, digital-only terms and variants; do not add the counts together.
The local broader master inventory is Wiki-derived. Its 224 unmatched names are leads only, include combined names/aliases and effect families, and are not official verification.

First: address the Jump association ambiguity alongside the next small mapping pass. Keep card-specific references tied to verified card examples.
Next: the 20 exact reported-label proposals below. They fit the current reviewed-registry approach. Exact official wording is already retained locally. New mappings still need positive/negative regression fixtures and normal integration checks before activation.
After that: separately scope non-keyword concepts such as Monarch, Initiative, energy, tokens and descended. Definitions alone cannot make the current keywords-only detector see them. Do not quietly turn on broad Oracle-text parsing in a label-mapping change.
Later: verify individual card-specific labels through official Gatherer/card images, and leave digital, acorn, obsolete and unreleased mechanics lower priority for this Commander-focused experience. A card-specific label may correctly have no mechanic-wide search.

HOW UNKNOWN MECHANICS CURRENTLY FAIL GRACEFULLY
1. Import stays intact. Unknown reported labels and their contributing cards remain available with images and Oracle text.
2. The UI says: No linked explanation is available in this rules reference. Open a card below to read its rules.
3. The UI says: No validated discovery search is linked to this provider label yet. Its imported cards remain available; new labels require an explicit reviewed mapping.
4. No search button is offered without an approved registry entry. The server rejects unknown mappings and arbitrary client queries; labels never become queries automatically.
5. Missing/changed official rules fail closed while keeping imported cards available. Missing or malformed keyword arrays are disclosed separately from empty arrays.
6. The blind spot: a mechanic omitted from keywords[] does not get an individual unknown-label warning because no label reaches the app. The card remains readable and the page warns that its list may not include every mechanic. Court of Grace has no keywords despite Monarch text; Aether Hub has energy text but no keywords. Fixing that coverage would be a separate, bounded detection change.

- No explanation is invented; no Search action or network prefetch appears
- An unknown or malicious query label cannot execute at the server

Daybound / Nightbound
- Arlinn, the Pack’s Hope has both labels; two display entries share the official section without inferring which face currently applies

Commander variants
- Yuriko is Commander ninjutsu; Ninja of the Deep Hours is ordinary Ninjutsu

Multikicker and typecycling
- Everflowing Chalice is Multikicker; ordinary Kicker remains separate
- Absorb Vis is Basic landcycling; explicitly enumerate the eight proposed cycling variants
- Retain labels, printing provenance, and quantities when one card reports family and variant labels

Descend semantics
- Basking Capybara has Descend and a graveyard-count threshold
- Broodrage Mycoid has no provider keyword but refers to having descended this turn; do not equate this with Descend 4/8

Unreported concepts
- Court of Grace has no keywords but establishes Monarch
- Aether Hub has no keywords but uses energy
- Academy Manufactor reports Food/Treasure but not Clue
- Radstorm has Storm/Proliferate and must not become a rad-counter card based on its name

Text-query false positives
- o:absorb finds Absorbing Man; narrowed absorb-number expression excludes it
- A word-boundary voting query excludes the extra match returned by o:vote
- Clue references and Investigate must both be covered; direct Clue mentions alone are incomplete
- Token queries must not turn every namesake card into a token mechanic

Provider ambiguity
- Direct Current reports Jump and Jump-start, but its rules contain only Jump-start
- Do not apply Cid’s card-specific Jump wording universally to all provider Jump labels

Failure states
- Missing, malformed, and empty keyword metadata remain distinct
- Missing rules/catalog resources keep imported cards readable
- Zero matches differs from provider failure; do not broaden commander colors to obtain results
- Unverified official wording stays flagged; revised sources fail closed rather than silently accepting drift
