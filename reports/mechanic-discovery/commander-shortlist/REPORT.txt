Data safeguards
Rules still require the pinned September 25, 2026 source revision. Direct links
also require the exact heading; explanations require unchanged numbered clauses.
Ability-word summaries give orientation and defer to the card's exact conditions.
Corrupted's ability-word status is additionally supported by Wizards' mechanics
article (the retained CR 207.2c list omits it):
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/phyrexia-all-will-be-one-mechanics
Prepared's designation is defined in retained CR 722.3a-c and supported by:
https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/secrets-of-strixhaven-mechanics

Searches use reviewed literal expressions, never interpolate imported labels,
require explicit clicks, preserve the commander color ceiling, and do not infer
inherent possession, an originating card face, or legal commander pairing.

recorded in baseline_case/baseline_relation. The UI uses current response counts.
